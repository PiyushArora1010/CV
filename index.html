Piyush Arora

MSc Computing (AI & ML), Imperial College London

I am a graduate researcher working on deployable machine learning systems, at the intersection of multimodal models, speech and vision-language learning, and LLM post-training. I am pursuing an MSc in Computing (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning) at Imperial College London, after a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Jodhpur. My work has been published at INTERSPEECH, EMNLP, and WACV.

Most recently I was a Machine Learning Intern at Neuphonic, working on post-training and distillation of audio language models for noise-robust speech recognition. Before graduate study, I was a Research Engineer at MetaFusion, building and deploying vision-language systems for traffic analytics.
