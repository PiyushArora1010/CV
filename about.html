Profile

I am a graduate researcher pursuing an MSc in Computing (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning) at Imperial College London, where I have achieved a Distinction in coursework. I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Jodhpur. My research spans multimodal learning, speech and vision-language modelling, and LLM post-training, with an emphasis on systems that can actually be deployed.

My work has been published at INTERSPEECH 2026 (oral), EMNLP 2025 and WACV 2024. Alongside research, I have industry experience as a Machine Learning Intern at Neuphonic, working on audio language models for noise-robust speech recognition, and as a Research Engineer at MetaFusion, where I deployed vision-language systems in production.

Current Focus

Recent and ongoing research at Imperial College London:

  • I-FAITH: reinforcement learning with counterfactual hint interventions to train LLMs to verbalise the factors that causally drive their predictions, improving chain-of-thought faithfulness.
  • Matry-FocalCodec: Matryoshka Representation Learning for neural audio codecs, so that one model supports multiple code dimensions and token rates.
  • VIB-AVSR: LLM-based audio-visual speech recognition with variational information bottleneck regularisation for noise robustness (INTERSPEECH 2026, oral).
  • StyleLip: few-shot speaking-style transfer for lip synchronisation using LoRA-based adaptation and a new style-consistency metric.

Technical Focus

I work primarily in Python, C++, SQL and Bash, with PyTorch, TensorFlow, Hugging Face Transformers, Scikit-learn and OpenCV. My post-training experience covers supervised fine-tuning and RL-based training (GRPO, GSPO, LUSPO) with reward design, ablations and benchmarking. I also deploy ML services with Docker, Kubernetes and Linux on GPU-based infrastructure.
