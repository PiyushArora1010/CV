Improving Chain-of-Thought Faithfulness in LLMs

Oct 2025 – Sep 2026

Imperial College London · Supervisors: Dr. Oana-Maria Camburu, Dr. María Pérez-Ortiz, Noah Y. Siegel · Code

  • Developed I-FAITH, a reinforcement learning algorithm that uses counterfactual hint interventions to train LLMs to identify and verbalise the factors that causally influence their predictions, addressing a key AI-safety challenge in chain-of-thought monitoring.
  • Designed a frequency-based simulation reward with an adaptive answer-consistency constraint, mitigating reward hacking and balancing faithful reasoning against task accuracy using LUSPO-based policy optimisation.
  • Evaluated on Qwen3-4B, 8B and 14B across SNLI, ETHICS, and out-of-distribution loan-approval, diabetes-diagnosis and admissions tasks; improved average OOD faithfulness GMean from 29.4% to 58.7% and generalised to six unseen hint types.
  • Reinforcement Learning
  • LLM Post-Training
  • Faithfulness
  • AI Safety

Matryoshka-Based Neural Codec for Adaptive Speech Tokenization

Apr 2026 – Present

Imperial College London · Supervisors: Dr. Mirco Ravanelli, Dr. Umberto Cappellazzo, Dr. Stavros Petridis

  • Developed Matry-FocalCodec, extending FocalCodec with Matryoshka Representation Learning so that a single neural audio codec supports multiple configurations, instead of one separately trained model per setting.
  • Evaluated along two axes, code dimension and token rate, against separately trained FocalCodec baselines, retaining competitive performance across both within a single model.

VIB-AVSR: Noise-Robust LLM-Based Audio-Visual Speech Recognition

Nov 2025 – Mar 2026

Imperial College London · Supervisors: Dr. Umberto Cappellazzo, Dr. Stavros Petridis, Dr. Maja Pantic · Paper

  • Developed VIB-AVSR, an LLM-based audio-visual speech recognition model connecting pre-trained audio-visual encoders to an LLM backbone.
  • Integrated Variational Information Bottleneck layers at targeted positions within the LLM backbone to regularise representations, addressing the lack of explicit noise-robustness mechanisms in existing LLM-based AVSR models.
  • Reduced degradation across SNR levels and noise types without architectural changes or additional training data. Published at INTERSPEECH 2026 (Oral).
  • Audio-Visual Speech Recognition
  • LLMs
  • Information Bottleneck

StyleLip: Cross-Identity Speaking Style Transfer

Oct 2025 – Mar 2026

Imperial College London · Supervisors: Dr. Antoni Bigata, Dr. Stavros Petridis, Dr. Maja Pantic

  • Developed StyleLip, a few-shot framework for speaking-style transfer in audio-driven lip synchronisation that reproduces person-specific lip movements across identities using LoRA layers in a base lip-sync model.
  • Regularised training with synthetic multi-identity samples sharing a speaking style to prevent identity leakage, achieving state-of-the-art performance as a plug-and-play solution.
  • Introduced SSCS, a quantitative metric for speaking-style transfer fidelity.
  • LoRA
  • Lip Synchronisation
  • Style Transfer

When Big Models Train Small Ones

Dec 2024 – Mar 2025

IIT Jodhpur, VL2G Lab · Supervisor: Dr. Anand Mishra · Paper

  • Designed Model Parity Alignment (MPA) to train small VLMs (SmolVLM-500M, TinyLLaVA-2B, InternVL2-2B/4B) from large VLMs (Qwen2VL-7B, InternVL2-8B, GPT-4o) using unlabelled data.
  • Developed disparity-aware training that closes reasoning gaps and mitigates hallucination via automated QA-pair generation and filtering of disparity samples across OCR, commonsense and factual tasks.
  • Achieved accuracy gains of up to 6% on TextVQA, ST-VQA, OKVQA, MedicalVQA and ChartQA. Published at EMNLP 2025.
  • Vision-Language Models
  • Knowledge Transfer
  • VQA

Hybrid Sample Synthesis-Based Debiasing in Limited Data Settings

Dec 2022 – Mar 2023

IIT Jodhpur · Supervisor: Dr. Pratik Mazumder · Paper

  • Designed a hybrid sample synthesis method for low-data regimes, enabling classifiers to generalise under unknown biases.
  • Built a dual-model framework that creates bias-conflicting samples without explicit bias annotations.
  • Achieved up to 10% accuracy improvement over prior methods on Corrupted CIFAR-10, Colored MNIST and BFFHQ. Published at WACV 2024.
